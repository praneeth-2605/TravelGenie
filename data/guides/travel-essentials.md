# Travel Planning Essentials

## Budget planning
Split a trip budget into: transport to and from the destination, accommodation, local transport, food, activities and entry fees, and a 10–15% buffer for surprises. Accommodation and flights usually take 50–70% of the total. Booking flights 6–10 weeks ahead for short-haul and 2–4 months ahead for long-haul often gets better fares, and mid-week flights are frequently cheaper.

### Budget styles
- **Backpacker**: hostels or guesthouses, street food, public transport, free sights.
- **Mid-range**: 3-star hotels or apartments, a mix of local restaurants and sit-down meals, some paid tours.
- **Luxury**: 4–5-star hotels, private transfers, fine dining, guided experiences.

## Itinerary pacing
Plan 2–3 major activities per day at most, grouped by neighbourhood to cut travel time. Leave the first afternoon after a long flight light. Put weather-dependent activities (beaches, viewpoints, hikes) early in the trip so they can be moved if it rains. Keep one flexible half-day on trips longer than four days.

## Packing list basics
- Documents: passport (valid 6+ months beyond your return for many countries), visas, insurance details, booking confirmations, offline copies of all of them.
- Money: two payment cards kept in separate places, some local cash.
- Electronics: phone, charger, plug adapter, power bank.
- Health: prescription medicines in their original packaging, basic first aid, sunscreen, insect repellent for tropical destinations.
- Clothing: layers rather than bulk; one smarter outfit; comfortable walking shoes; a rain jacket or umbrella for monsoon or rainy seasons; modest clothing for religious sites.

## Weather-based packing
- Hot and humid (above 30°C): light breathable cotton or linen, hat, electrolyte sachets.
- Rainy season: quick-dry clothes, waterproof phone pouch, sandals.
- Cold (below 10°C): thermal base layer, warm jacket, gloves, beanie.

## Safety and health
Get travel insurance that covers medical evacuation. Check your government's travel advisory for the destination. Share your itinerary with someone at home. Use official taxis or ride-hailing apps. Check whether routine vaccinations are up to date and if the destination needs others.

## Money tips
Tell your bank you're travelling. When a card terminal offers to charge in your home currency, choose the local currency instead; the conversion offered is usually worse. Use ATMs inside banks where possible.

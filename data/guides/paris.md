# Paris

## Overview
Paris is divided into 20 arrondissements spiralling out from the centre. The Seine splits it into the Right Bank (Louvre, Marais, Montmartre) and the Left Bank (Saint-Germain, Latin Quarter, Eiffel Tower).

## When to go
- **April to June** and **September to October**: mild weather, long days; the best balance.
- **July to August**: warm and busy with tourists, while many locals leave in August and some small shops close.
- **November to February**: cold and grey, but fewer crowds and lower hotel prices; Christmas markets and lights in December.

## Getting around
- The Métro is fast and covers the city densely. Contactless bank cards work on the gates, or use a Navigo Easy pass topped up with tickets.
- From CDG airport: RER B train to the centre, or a fixed-fare taxi to the Right or Left Bank.
- Paris is very walkable; Vélib' shared bikes are another option.

## Major sights
### Louvre
Book a timed entry online; closed on Tuesdays. The Richelieu wing is often quieter than the Denon wing (Mona Lisa).
### Eiffel Tower
Book summit tickets ahead or take the stairs to the second floor. The best photos are from Trocadéro; the tower sparkles for five minutes every hour after dark.
### Musée d'Orsay
Impressionist collection in a former railway station; closed on Mondays.
### Montmartre
Sacré-Cœur basilica and artists' square Place du Tertre. Visit early to avoid crowds and watch for petition scams near the steps.
### Notre-Dame
Reopened in December 2024 after restoration; entry is free, with optional reservation slots to skip the queue.

## Neighbourhoods
- **Le Marais**: boutiques, falafel on Rue des Rosiers, Place des Vosges.
- **Saint-Germain-des-Prés**: classic cafés (Café de Flore, Les Deux Magots), bookshops.
- **Canal Saint-Martin**: young, local crowd and picnics by the canal.

## Food
Bakeries for croissants and baguettes, bistros for steak-frites and duck confit, crêperies, and cheese shops (fromageries). Lunch prix-fixe menus (formule) are the best value. Service is included in the bill; leaving a little extra is optional.

## Day trips
- **Versailles**: the palace and gardens, 40 minutes by RER C. Closed on Mondays; book ahead.
- **Giverny**: Monet's gardens, April to October.

## Budget tips
Many museums are free on the first Sunday of some months, and free for EU residents under 26. The Paris Museum Pass helps if you visit many museums in a few days. Picnics with market food along the Seine are cheap and classic.

## Safety
Pickpockets work on the Métro (especially Line 1), around the Eiffel Tower and the Louvre. Keep bags zipped and in front of you.
